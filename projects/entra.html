01 / SECURITY PROBLEM

The challenge

Privileged access, guest permissions, service principal credentials, and broad RBAC assignments are difficult to assess consistently without a unified review queue.

02 / SOLUTION DESIGN

The approach

Built read-only Microsoft Graph/Azure ARM collectors, nine policy-oriented review controls, role drift comparison, attestation decisions, independent approvals, and expiry-aware exceptions.

03 / MEASURABLE FINDING

What the evidence shows

An evaluation using 2,000 generated users and 376 role/credential records surfaced 138 distinct first-pass review items, with 119 principals requiring potential reviewer outreach.

04 / OPERATIONAL DECISION

What this informs

Route possible exceptions to accountable reviewers rather than treating flagged access as confirmed violations; preserve owner and evidence decisions.
