Trade-offs / known limitations

  • Labels and thresholds are constructed for a repeatable exercise; scenario prevalence is not representative of a real tenant.
  • Shared proxy traffic remains ambiguous; the tuned model can still alert on legitimate shared-network failures.
  • Low-and-slow password spray and low-volume takeover cases are missed by both candidate policies.
  • Python scenario checks are not a KQL execution engine or a production incident outcome measurement.
